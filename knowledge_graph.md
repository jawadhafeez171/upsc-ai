--------------------------------------------------
CORE PHILOSOPHY
--------------------------------------------------

The Knowledge Graph is the single source of truth.

Everything else will be attached to the graph.

Do not design around AI.

Design around knowledge.

--------------------------------------------------
DESIGN PRINCIPLES
--------------------------------------------------

1. Every concept exists only once.

Never duplicate nodes.

2. Relationships connect knowledge.

Do not duplicate information simply because it appears in multiple papers.

3. Follow the official UPSC syllabus.

Expand the syllabus into a structured knowledge graph.

4. Think long term.

The graph should eventually support:

- UPSC
- KPSC/KAS
- SSC
- Banking
- State PSCs

5. Every node must be reusable.

Example:

"Brihadeeswara Temple"

must not exist under

History

and

Art & Culture

as two different nodes.

Instead it should exist once and be connected to both.
