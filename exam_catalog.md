## Tier 1: Central Civil Services & Defence (UPSC)

### 1. UPSC Civil Services Examination (CSE)
- **Exam Stages**:
  - **Prelims**: Paper-1 (General Studies - 100 Qs · 200 Marks) + Paper-2 (CSAT - 80 Qs · 200 Marks · Qualifying 33%)
  - **Mains**: Essay + GS-1 + GS-2 + GS-3 + GS-4 + 2 Optional Papers
  - **Personality Test**: 275 Marks
- **Key Knowledge Pillars**: History, Polity, Geography, Economy, Environment, S&T, IR, Security, Society, Ethics, CSAT.

### 2. UPSC Central Armed Police Forces (CAPF - AC)
- **Target Posts**: Assistant Commandant in BSF, CRPF, CISF, ITBP, SSB
- **Exam Stages**:
  - **Paper-1 (General Ability & Intelligence)**: 125 MCQs · 250 Marks (General Science, Current Events, Indian History, Indian & World Geography, Indian Polity & Economy, GMA/Reasoning).
  - **Paper-2 (General Studies, Essay & Comprehension)**: 200 Marks descriptive (Essay in English/Hindi, Precis, Comprehension, Counter-Arguments, Reports, Grammar).
  - **Physical Efficiency Test (PET)** & Interview.
- **Key Knowledge Pillars**: Modern History, Freedom Struggle, Polity & Internal Security, Defence Tech, Geography, GMA.

### 3. UPSC Combined Defence Services (CDS)
- **Target Academies**: IMA (Indian Military Academy), INA (Naval Academy), AFA (Air Force Academy), OTA (Officers Training Academy)
- **Exam Stages**:
  - **IMA / INA / AFA**: English (100 Marks) + General Knowledge (100 Marks) + Elementary Mathematics (100 Marks)
  - **OTA**: English (100 Marks) + General Knowledge (100 Marks)
  - **SSB Interview**: 5-Day Psychological, GTO & Personal Interview.
- **Key Knowledge Pillars**: General Science (Physics, Chemistry, Biology), History, Geography, Polity, Defence/Current Affairs, Elementary Mathematics.

### 4. UPSC National Defence Academy (NDA & NA)
- **Eligibility**: 10+2 (Army, Navy, Air Force wings)
- **Exam Stages**:
  - **Paper-1 (Mathematics)**: 120 Qs · 300 Marks (Algebra, Trigonometry, Matrices, Calculus, Vectors, Statistics, Probability).
  - **Paper-2 (General Ability Test - GAT)**: 150 Qs · 600 Marks (Part A: English - 200 Marks; Part B: General Knowledge - 400 Marks: Physics, Chemistry, General Science, History/Freedom Movement, Geography, Current Events).
- **Key Knowledge Pillars**: 10+2 Mathematics, General Science, World & Indian Geography, Freedom Struggle.

### 5. UPSC EPFO (EO/AO & APFC)
- **Target Posts**: Enforcement Officer / Accounts Officer (EO/AO) & Assistant Public Provident Commissioner (APFC)
- **Exam Stages**:
  - **Recruitment Test (RT)**: 120 MCQs · 300 Marks + Interview (100 Marks)
- **Key Syllabus Modules**: General English, Indian Freedom Struggle, Current Events, Indian Polity & Economy, **General Accounting Principles**, **Industrial Relations & Labour Laws**, General Science & Knowledge of Computer Applications, GMA & Quantitative Aptitude, **Social Security in India**, Auditing & Insurance (APFC).

### 6. UPSC Combined Geo-Scientist / Engineering Services (ESE GS)
- **Paper-1 (Stage-1)**: General Studies and Engineering Aptitude (200 Marks · 100 Qs: Current Issues, Engineering Ethics, Environmental Pollution, Project Management, ICT).

## Tier 2: Regulatory Bodies & Central Banking

### 1. Reserve Bank of India (RBI Grade B - General)
- **Exam Stages**:
  - **Phase-1 (Objective)**: 200 Qs · 200 Marks · 120 Mins
    - General Awareness (80 Qs · 80 Marks)
    - Quantitative Aptitude (30 Qs · 30 Marks)
    - Reasoning Ability (60 Qs · 60 Marks)
    - English Language (30 Qs · 30 Marks)
  - **Phase-2 (Descriptive + Objective)**: 300 Marks
    - **Paper-1**: Economic & Social Issues (ESI - 50% Obj + 50% Desc)
    - **Paper-2**: English Writing Skills (100 Marks Desc - Essay, Precis, Reading Comprehension)
    - **Paper-3**: Finance and Management (F&M - 50% Obj + 50% Desc: Financial System, Monetary Policy, Corporate Governance, Leadership, Motivation, Communication, HRD)
  - **Phase-3**: Interview (75 Marks)
- **Key Knowledge Pillars**: Macroeconomics, Indian Banking System, Financial Markets, Management Theories, Union Budget, Economic Survey, GMA.

### 2. SEBI Grade A (Assistant Manager - General)
- **Phase-1**: Paper-1 (GA, Quant, Reasoning, English - 100 Marks) + Paper-2 (Commerce, Accountancy, Management, Finance, Costing, Companies Act, Economics - 100 Marks).
- **Phase-2**: Paper-1 (English Descriptive - 100 Marks) + Paper-2 (Specialized Core Stream - 100 Marks: Securities Market Regulations, Financial System, Economics).

### 3. NABARD Grade A (RDBS - Rural Development Banking Service)
- **Phase-1**: Reasoning, English, Computer Knowledge, Quantitative Aptitude, Decision Making, General Awareness, **Economic & Social Issues (ESI)**, **Agriculture & Rural Development (ARD with focus on Rural India)**.
- **Phase-2**: Paper-1 (General English Descriptive) + Paper-2 (ESI and ARD).

### 4. IFSCA Grade A (International Financial Services Centres Authority) & IRDAI AM
- **Syllabus Focus**: Global Financial Regulations, Insurance Architecture, Capital Markets, Macroeconomics, Union Budget.

## Tier 3: Karnataka State Government Examinations

### A. KPSC Examinations (Karnataka Public Service Commission)

#### 1. KPSC KAS (Karnataka Administrative Service - Group A & B Gazetted Probationers)
- **Prelims (400 Marks · 200 Qs)**:
  - **Paper 1 (200 Marks)**: Current Events (National/Intl), Indian History, World & **Karnataka History**, Indian & **Karnataka Geography**, Indian Polity & **Karnataka Governance**, Economic & Social Development, **Karnataka Economy**.
  - **Paper 2 (200 Marks)**: State Current Affairs, General Science & Tech, Environment & Ecology, General Mental Ability, Quantitative Aptitude, Data Interpretation, Reading Comprehension (Kannada & English).
- **Mains (7 Descriptive Papers)**:
  - Compulsory Kannada & English (Qualifying)
  - Essay (250 Marks)
  - GS-1: History & Heritage (India & Karnataka), Social & Political Perspective, Indian & Karnataka Economy, Data Analysis (250 Marks).
  - GS-2: Geography & Natural Resources (India & Karnataka), Indian Constitution, Public Admin & Management, IR (250 Marks).
  - GS-3: Science & Tech in Development, Environmental Ecology of India & Karnataka (250 Marks).
  - GS-4: Ethics, Integrity & Aptitude (250 Marks).

#### 2. KPSC Group C Non-Technical (Commercial Tax Inspector - CTI, FDA, SDA, ESI)
- **Target Posts**: Commercial Tax Inspector (CTI), First Division Assistant (FDA), Second Division Assistant (SDA), Excise Sub-Inspector (ESI), Bill Collector.
- **Exam Pattern (2 Objective Papers · 100 Marks each)**:
  - **Paper 1 (General Knowledge - 100 Marks · 90 Mins)**: Current Affairs, General Science, Indian History, Indian Geography, Indian Polity, Indian Economy, Everyday Science, Karnataka State History, Geography & Schemes.
  - **Paper 2 (General Kannada / General English / Computer Knowledge - 100 Marks · 120 Mins)**:
    - General Kannada (35 Marks)
    - General English (35 Marks)
    - Computer Knowledge (30 Marks)

#### 3. KPSC Panchayat Development Officer (PDO) & GPS
- **Exam Pattern**:
  - **Paper 1**: General Knowledge, Karnataka GK, Current Affairs, Kannada, English, Computer Literacy.
  - **Paper 2 (Specialized Paper)**: **Karnataka Gram Swaraj and Panchayat Raj Act 1993** (all chapters, amendments, powers of Gram Panchayat, Taluk Panchayat, Zilla Panchayat), Rural Development and Panchayat Raj Schemes (Mahatma Gandhi NREGA, NRLM, Jal Jeevan Mission, SBM-G, PMAY-G, Swachh Bharat).

#### 4. KPSC Assistant Conservator of Forests (ACF) & Range Forest Officer (RFO)
- **Prelims**: Paper-1 (General Studies) + Paper-2 (General Aptitude).
- **Mains**: Compulsory Kannada/English, General Knowledge, and Forestry / Botany / Zoology / Agricultural Sciences.

#### 2. KSP Police Constable (PC - Civil, CAR/DAR, Armed Police)
- **Exam Pattern (Objective · 100 Qs · 100 Marks · 120 Mins)**:
  - General Knowledge, Science, Geography, History, Indian Constitution, Everyday Mental Ability, Basic Arithmetic, Current Affairs.

## Tier 4: Staff Selection Commission (SSC)

### 1. SSC CGL (Combined Graduate Level)
- **Tier-1 (Computer Based · 100 Qs · 200 Marks · 60 Mins)**:
  - General Intelligence & Reasoning (25 Qs · 50 Marks)
  - General Awareness (25 Qs · 50 Marks)
  - Quantitative Aptitude (25 Qs · 50 Marks)
  - English Comprehension (25 Qs · 50 Marks)
- **Tier-2 (Computer Based · Merit)**:
  - **Section I**: Mathematical Abilities (30 Qs) + Reasoning & General Intelligence (30 Qs) [60 Qs · 180 Marks]
  - **Section II**: English Language & Comprehension (45 Qs) + General Awareness (25 Qs) [70 Qs · 210 Marks]
  - **Section III**: Computer Knowledge Module (20 Qs · 60 Marks · Qualifying) + Data Entry Speed Test (DEST).

### 2. SSC CHSL (10+2 Level)
- **Tier-1**: English Language, General Intelligence, Quantitative Aptitude (Basic Arithmetic Skills), General Awareness.
- **Tier-2**: Mathematical Abilities, Reasoning, English & GA, Skill/Typing Test.

### 3. SSC CPO (Sub-Inspector in Delhi Police & CAPFs)
- **Paper-1 (200 Qs · 200 Marks)**: General Intelligence & Reasoning, General Knowledge & General Awareness, Quantitative Aptitude, English Comprehension.
- **Paper-2 (200 Qs · 200 Marks)**: English Language & Comprehension.

### 4. SSC MTS (Multi-Tasking Staff) & Havaldar
- **Session 1**: Numerical & Mathematical Ability, Reasoning Ability & Problem Solving.
- **Session 2**: General Awareness, English Language & Comprehension.

## Tier 7: Teaching Eligibility & Faculty Selection Examinations

### A. Karnataka State Teaching Examinations

#### 1. KARTET (Karnataka Teachers Eligibility Test)
- **Conducted By**: School Education Department, Karnataka.
- **Paper 1 (For Classes 1 to 5 - Primary Teachers · 150 MCQs · 150 Marks)**:
  - Child Development & Pedagogy (CDP - 30 Qs · 30 Marks)
  - Language I (Kannada / Urdu / Tamil / Telugu / Marathi / Hindi / English - 30 Qs · 30 Marks)
  - Language II (English - 30 Qs · 30 Marks)
  - Mathematics (30 Qs · 30 Marks)
  - Environmental Studies (EVS - 30 Qs · 30 Marks)
- **Paper 2 (For Classes 6 to 8 - Higher Primary Teachers · 150 MCQs · 150 Marks)**:
  - Child Development & Pedagogy (30 Qs · 30 Marks)
  - Language I (30 Qs · 30 Marks)
  - Language II (30 Qs · 30 Marks)
  - **Subject Stream (60 Qs · 60 Marks)**:
    - *Maths & Science Stream*: Mathematics (30 Qs) + General Science (30 Qs)
    - *Social Studies Stream*: History, Geography, Polity, Social Life (60 Qs)

#### 2. Karnataka GPSTR (Graduate Primary School Teacher Recruitment - Classes 6 to 8)
- **Paper 1 (General Studies & Education · 150 Marks)**:
  - General Kannada (25 Marks)
  - General English (25 Marks)
  - General Knowledge & Current Affairs (25 Marks - Indian & Karnataka History, Constitution, Geography, Science)
  - Educational Psychology, Child Rights & Inclusive Education (50 Marks)
  - Computer Literacy & Value Education (25 Marks)
- **Paper 2 (Specific Subject · 150 Marks)**:
  - Mathematics & Science, Social Science (History, Geography, Polity, Economics), or Languages.
- **Paper 3 (Descriptive Kannada Language Proficiency Test · 100 Marks)**.

#### 3. Karnataka HSTR (High School Teacher Recruitment - Classes 9 & 10)
- **Paper 1 (General Paper · 100 Marks)**: General Knowledge, General Science, Indian Constitution, Educational Psychology & Pedagogy, Computer Awareness, General Kannada & English.
- **Paper 2 (Specific Subject Content & Pedagogy · 100 Marks)**: Physics/Maths, Chemistry/Biology, Social Science, English, Kannada.

#### 4. KEA Assistant Professor & KSET (Karnataka State Eligibility Test)
- **Paper 1 (General Paper on Teaching & Research Aptitude · 50 Qs · 100 Marks · 60 Mins)**:
  - Teaching Aptitude (Learner characteristics, teaching methods, evaluation systems).
  - Research Aptitude (Methods of research, thesis writing, research ethics).
  - Reading Comprehension.
  - Communication & ICT (Information & Communication Technology).
  - Mathematical Reasoning & Data Interpretation.
  - People, Development & Environment (Millennium & Sustainable Development Goals, Environmental pollution, Climate Change).
  - Higher Education System (Governance, polity and administration in higher education).
- **Paper 2 (Subject Specific · 100 Qs · 200 Marks · 120 Mins)**.

#### 5. Karnataka Pre-University College Lecturer Recruitment (PUE Lecturers)
- **Paper 1**: General Studies (GK, Mental Ability, Indian Constitution, Karnataka Culture).
- **Paper 2**: Post-Graduate Level Core Subject.

## 🎯 Implementation Roadmap for MockIQ

1. **Phase 1 (Current)**:
   - Full Coverage of **UPSC CSE** (Prelims GS-1/CSAT + Mains GS1–4).
   - Full Coverage of **KPSC KAS** (Prelims Paper 1 & 2 + Mains 7 Papers).

2. **Phase 2 (Karnataka State Expansion + Defence Focus)**:
   - **KEA Examinations**:
     - **KEA VAO** (Village Administrative Officer / Revenue Dept).
     - **KEA / KPSC PDO** (Panchayat Development Officer & Gram Swaraj 1993 Act).
     - **KEA Assistant Professor & KSET** (General Paper 1).
     - **KEA Power PSUs** (BESCOM, KPTCL, GESCOM Junior Assistant).
   - **Karnataka State Police (KSP)**:
     - **KSP PSI** (Police Sub-Inspector - Civil, CAR/DAR, KSISF - Paper 1 Translation/Essay + Paper 2 GS).
     - **KSP PC** (Police Constable).
   - **KPSC Group C Non-Technical**:
     - **CTI** (Commercial Tax Inspector), **ESI** (Excise Sub-Inspector), **FDA**, **SDA**.
   - **Karnataka Teaching Examinations**:
     - **KARTET** (Paper 1 & Paper 2 Social Science / Maths-Science).
     - **Karnataka GPSTR** (Paper 1 GS & Educational Psychology + Paper 2).
     - **Karnataka HSTR** (High School Teachers).
   - **UPSC Central Defence**:
     - **UPSC CAPF (AC)** (Paper 1 & Paper 2).
     - **UPSC CDS** (IMA, INA, AFA, OTA).

3. **Phase 3 (Central SSC, Regulatory & National Teaching Focus)**:
   - **Regulatory Bodies**: **RBI Grade B** (Phase 1 + Phase 2 ESI / F&M), **SEBI Grade A**, **NABARD Grade A (ARD/ESI)**.
   - **Staff Selection Commission**: **SSC CGL** (Tier 1 & Tier 2), **SSC CHSL**, **SSC CPO**.
   - **National Teaching**: **CTET** (Paper 1 & 2), **UGC NET** (Paper 1), **KVS / NVS / EMRS**.

4. **Phase 4 (Banking, Insurance & Railways Mega Scale)**:
   - **Banking**: **SBI PO & Clerk**, **IBPS PO & Clerk**, **IBPS RRB (PO & Clerk)**.
   - **Insurance PSUs**: **LIC AAO / ADO**, **NIACL AO**, **UIIC AO**.
   - **Railways**: **RRB NTPC** (Graduate & Undergraduate CBT-1 & 2), **RRB Group D**, **RRB ALP**.
