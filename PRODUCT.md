# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Serious UPSC CSE and State PCS aspirants preparing for Prelims (GS Paper 1, CSAT Paper 2) and Mains. They engage in intensive daily practice, timed Computer-Based Test (CBT) mock simulations, and systematic revision across both desktop workstations and mobile study sessions.

## Product Purpose

To provide serious civil services aspirants with an authoritative, distraction-free preparation and assessment environment. Success means candidates master exam patterns, develop reliable question-solving stamina, and target weak areas with granular precision.

## Positioning

A comprehensive, high-precision archive of 30+ years of official UPSC Civil Services Preliminary Examination questions (1995–2024), organized with granular subject, topic, and difficulty categorization, paired with rigorous explanations and exam-accurate CBT simulation.

## Operating Context

- Timed, high-stakes mock test environments (120-minute GS, 80-question CSAT) with official-style question palettes, mark-for-review workflows, and negative marking rules.
- Deep study sessions involving long-form comprehension passages (English & Hindi) and complex multi-statement assertions.
- Daily revision habits across mobile devices (commute/quick review) and desktop screens (full CBT exam drills).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Bilingual support (English and Hindi) for question papers, options, and explanations.
- Math and scientific notation rendering via KaTeX.
- Real-time exam timer, question navigation grid, and instant post-test diagnostic analytics.
- Granular filtering across 30+ years by subject (Polity, History, Economy, Geography, Environment, Science & Tech, Art & Culture, CSAT), topic, and difficulty.
- Zero visual fluff or distracting "gamification" that compromises serious academic focus.

## Brand Commitments

- **Name**: UPSC AI
- **Tone**: Academic, authoritative, restrained, dependable, and distraction-free.
- **Visual Stance**: High editorial legibility, excellent contrast for sustained reading, clean data density, and authentic exam layout discipline.

## Evidence on Hand

- Supabase question database holding verified PYQ datasets spanning 1995–2024 across all GS subjects and CSAT.
- Working CBT testing engine with mobile and desktop responsive views.
- Bilingual translation and formatting infrastructure in `QuestionFormatter.tsx` and `src/lib/i18n.ts`.

## Product Principles

1. **Sustained Reading Comfort First**: Exam prep involves reading thousands of dense, multi-statement questions. Typography, contrast, and spacing must reduce cognitive fatigue over multi-hour sessions.
2. **Authentic Exam Integrity**: Testing flows must mirror real-world exam conditions and official CBT conventions rather than generic gamified quiz apps.
3. **Data Accuracy Over Decoration**: Question categorization, answer keys, citations, and analytical breakdowns must be indisputably precise. Visuals serve clarity, never decoration.
4. **Distraction-Free Speed**: Fast question navigation, zero layout shifts, and instantaneous state transitions to keep aspirants in a state of deep focus.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- High-contrast compliant typography for dark and light modes.
- Bilingual parity (Hindi & English) with proper Devanagari font scaling and line-height adjustments.
- Full mobile-touch target compliance (minimum 44px) and keyboard navigation support for test taking.
